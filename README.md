# tausocial
Social Media with accountability like never before, monitored by intelligence


A social media with:

- Accountability, ID is associated with govt id
- Your content is cleaned before it is posted. 
- You can share video, audio, text and have threats but its total clean, ethical - content will be auto checked for any invalid or indecent content
- Lie, fake content, influencing social thinking will be autoblocked 
- Nudity, personal information or leak of privacy is immediately blocked
- You can create content, but it has to be tagged as artificial, intent is to be tagged. No fake news, any content which is tagged as news and its semantics matches will be allowed as it is tagged as fake, or its intent is added as tag.
- Purest form of social media, with accountability, accuracy, privacy and most importantly algo choice - new content only, old and new, never watch again same content, never show much of new content
- Algorithm is updated as per your choices, a deep eye on your choices you make, dynamic personalized algorithm (which is not provided by any social media today)
- Security and privacy (Your data will be your proprietary right) and only you will have access to delete it or keep it
- Experience of use, frontend and backend with ease of use like never before.
