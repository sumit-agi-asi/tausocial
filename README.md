# tausocial

TauSocial is a social media concept centered on accountability, accuracy, privacy,
ethical publishing, and user-controlled personalization.

## Core principles

- **Verified accountability**: every account is associated with a government ID.
- **Pre-publication content checks**: text, audio, and video are reviewed before posting.
- **Ethical and clean platform**: indecent, threatening, abusive, or otherwise invalid content is blocked.
- **Anti-misinformation guardrails**: lies, fake content, and manipulation of social thinking are automatically blocked.
- **Privacy protection**: nudity, personal information, and privacy leaks are immediately blocked.
- **Transparent AI content**: artificially created content must be tagged, including its intent.
- **No fake news without disclosure**: content presented as news must carry accurate fake/intent labels whenever applicable.
- **User-controlled algorithms**: users choose how discovery works, including preferences for new-only content, mixed feeds, and repeat-avoidance.
- **Dynamic personalization**: ranking adapts continuously to user choices instead of using a fixed opaque algorithm.
- **User data ownership**: user data remains under the user's control, including retention and deletion choices.
- **Security and privacy by design**: platform behavior should prioritize protection, consent, and safe defaults.
- **Ease of use**: both frontend and backend experiences should be simple, clear, and dependable.
